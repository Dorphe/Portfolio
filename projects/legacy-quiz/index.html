Legacy

Personalized Recommendations

Sperm freezing is a high-consideration, low-awareness product. Legacy needed a new way to educate and convert. A guided recommendation quiz converted at nearly 5x the rest of the website.

The short version

Challenges

  • How to test for a quiz experience with a niche audience
  • Designing a scoring logic for the right recommendation
  • Balancing education with information overload

Key learnings

  • Value-perception matters more than information density for drop-off
  • Curating test participants carefully yields quality data faster
  • Right information, right time, right context drives conversion

Results

Metric Result
Conversion rate vs. website 5×
Average Order Value vs. historical 2×
Share of total revenue (first 2 weeks) 10.4%
Users who completed experience and converted 89%

Context

Legacy sold through its Shop All page and various landing pages. Conversion sat at 1.5%, well below industry average. Sperm freezing is both high-consideration and unfamiliar. Standard e-commerce tactics don't apply.

Goals

Users want to understand What is sperm freezing? Why should I do it? Can it help me have a family in the future? How does my lifestyle or medical condition affect my ability to procreate?
Product wants to Teach users what sperm freezing is and why they should freeze, while making sure users trust Legacy to care for their sperm storage.
Business wants to Improve overall average order value and improve conversion rate over 1%.

The hypothesis

Educating users through a guided experience will lead them to finding the right solution in their sperm freezing journey.

The process

We started with a lo-fi prototype in Typeform. It yielded useful data, but testing with a niche audience proved difficult.

Legacy's customers are undergoing life-changing events: vasectomies, chemotherapy, gender transition. Our pool on UserTesting was small, and widening the net led to poor-quality data from low-intent participants.

We ran focused tests with a curated group of past and prospective customers, then moved to ship and collect field data.

Key design views

Splash screen

The splash screen introduces the quiz and sets expectations: less than 2 minutes, personalized results.

Question view

Each question includes an educational drawer explaining why we ask sensitive health questions. This builds trust and reduces drop-off at the most personal moments in the flow.

Recommendation view

The recommendation links each bundle item back to the user's answers, reinforcing that this is their personalized result. Below the recommendation, "What to Expect" and "Ways to Pay" sections remove any remaining friction before checkout.

The logic

The quiz has fewer than a dozen questions, but the scoring logic underneath is substantial.

Legacy's bundles are prescriptive, so I reverse-engineered the individual items into a scoring system with multiple triggers throughout the flow to ensure accurate recommendations.

Conclusion

The results validated the hypothesis:

Educating users through a guided experience will lead them to finding the right solution in their sperm freezing journey.
  • 5× conversion rate compared to the website
  • 2× average order value compared to historical
  • 10.4% of total revenue within two weeks of its soft launch
  • 89% of users who got to the end of the experience converted
