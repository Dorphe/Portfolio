Assets Sharing Platform

Game development teams are very siloed. Portfolio's mission was to help artists save time and energy by sharing and reusing assets between production teams — growing from a few people per production to hundreds of users across multiple productions.

The problem

Game development teams at Ubisoft are highly siloed. Artists on one production would create assets — characters, props, textures, animations — that another team was simultaneously building from scratch. There was no system to discover, share, or reuse the work already done.

This meant duplicated effort, inconsistent visual quality across titles, and enormous waste of creative labor.

Responsibilities

  • Led design of the internal assets sharing platform (Portfolio)
  • First product within the department to be designed with a design system in mind
  • Assisted UX with research and user testing

The solution

Portfolio was designed as a searchable, browseable library of game assets — organized by type (3D assets, 2D assets, animations, sounds) and production — that any artist or director across Ubisoft could access.

Collections Artists and directors adopted Collections into their workflows by sharing asset collections for reviews. This became a key social feature — teams could curate and surface their best work for others to discover and reuse.
Design system foundation Portfolio was the first product within the internal tools department to be designed with a shared design system in mind. This laid the groundwork for the broader design system initiative that would eventually scale across desktop and web tools at Ubisoft.

Key results

  • Artists and directors adopted Collections into their workflows by sharing asset collections for reviews
  • Usage of the platform went up from a few people per production to hundreds of users across multiple productions
  • Established the design system foundation that would later be adopted department-wide
